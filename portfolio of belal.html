about

I'm a full-stack web developer who cares most about what happens behind the screen — the routes, the data models, and the logic that keeps an application reliable under real use.

Most of my work starts from a broken manual process: orders tracked in chat messages, records kept in spreadsheets, payments handled by hand. I design the database, build the backend with Laravel, wire up the frontend, and hand back a system the client can actually run on their own.

I'm currently a third-year Computer Science student at Nile University, sharpening my full-stack skills through the NTI program and hands-on projects — and looking for opportunities to build real, production-ready products.
